# What it costs

Every figure on this page is a planning estimate. None of them is a quote. They come from the work each kind of move usually involves, and they have not yet been checked against a finished engagement.

## How the ranges work

The range depends on the count that drives the work: sites for hosting and PHP moves, databases for SQL and ERP moves, model sets for design files, systems for public-records work. The low end assumes a tidy inventory with few surprises. The high end assumes old versions, missing backups or code nobody maintains.

After the assessment you get a fixed quote for the agreed scope. The first conversation is free. You pay for the work delivered.
