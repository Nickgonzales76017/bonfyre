# ERP and business systems

For organizations moving between ERP systems, upgrading Odoo or Frappe/ERPNext across major versions, or bringing records out of an older business application.

## What moves

Customers, suppliers, items, price lists, open orders, invoices and their history, the chart of accounts, users and their roles, and the custom fields and reports your staff rely on.

## What we check first

- The source and destination systems and versions are named.
- Record types, custom fields and the workflows that change them are listed.
- Who may see and change each kind of record today, so permissions carry over rather than open up.
- A rollback point exists before the first record is loaded.

## How the result is checked

Records are loaded into a rehearsal copy and compared with the source: counts per record type, totals per account and period, and a sample of documents opened side by side. Records that cannot be mapped go on an exceptions list for your team to decide.
