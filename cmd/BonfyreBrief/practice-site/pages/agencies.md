# For agencies hosting client sites

For agencies and studios that host a few dozen client sites on one server and want them somewhere easier to look after.

## How a portfolio move works

We take the full list of sites first and sort them into groups: sites that can move as they are, sites that need a PHP upgrade first, and sites that need a decision from their owner. Each group gets its own estimate, so you can approve the easy ones and keep talking about the rest.

## What your clients notice

Ideally nothing. Each site is staged and checked on the new server before its DNS changes, and the switch for each site is scheduled with you. Mail is copied before and after the switch so nothing sent in between is lost.

## What you get back

A record of every site: where it lives now, what changed, what was checked, and who holds access. If you want us to keep looking after the sites afterwards, that is a separate agreement.
