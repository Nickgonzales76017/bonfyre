# Leaving Plesk

For owners of a Plesk server moving subscriptions and domains to another host or panel.

## What we work from

Plesk exposes an XML-RPC interface that can list subscriptions, domains, mail and databases, and Plesk publishes a PHP library for it. Several other panels can import Plesk backups, which gives more than one way to move an account.

## What the move covers

Subscriptions and their domains, files, databases, mailboxes, and scheduled tasks. Each subscription is staged and checked before its DNS changes.

## Before anything moves

An inventory of subscriptions, a backup of each that restores without the old server, and the DNS records for every domain with their current timings.
