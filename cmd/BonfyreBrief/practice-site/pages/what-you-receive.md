# What you receive

Each step leaves you with a document you can keep, share with your own staff, or hand to another provider.

## The assessment brief

Written before any access is granted. It lists the documents we read, quotes the passages that matter with a reference to each one, sets out what has to be true before the move, gives the estimate range and what it covers, names what is still missing, and lists the next steps we would take. It closes with what the brief cannot tell you. The [sample brief](/sample-assessment-brief) is one we wrote for a hosting move.

## Inventory

Every site, domain, mailbox, database, record type or model set in scope, as you gave it to us and as we found it once we had access. Differences between the two are marked.

## Compatibility and risk report

What carries over, what needs work, and what has no clear path, with the reason for each item.

## Rehearsal results

What happened when the move was run on a copy: the errors, the fixes, how long it took, and anything still open.

## Before-and-after comparison

The side-by-side check from step six, with each mismatch and what was decided about it.

## Cutover and reversal plan

The order of the switch, who does each step, the DNS records and their timings, and the restore that takes you back if needed. The restore is tried once before the real cutover, and the time it took is written down.

## Handover notes

Where everything lives now, who holds which access, what was changed and why, and what to watch in the first weeks.
