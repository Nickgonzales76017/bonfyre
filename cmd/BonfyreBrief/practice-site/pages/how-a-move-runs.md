# How a move runs

Every move follows the same order, whatever the old system is. Your live system stays as it is until you have read the plan and agreed to it in writing.

## 1. Assessment

You tell us what you run today: the hosting panel, the sites on it, the database engine, the kinds of records you keep. We read the vendor documentation and project notes that apply to that system and write down what has to be true before anything moves. Every statement in the assessment points to the document it came from.

## 2. Compatibility and risk report

With your inventory in hand we list what will carry over cleanly, what needs changes, and what has no clear path. Plugins nobody maintains, stored procedures with no equivalent, rules that depend on the date a case was opened, and mail that has to keep arriving during the move each get named here.

## 3. Estimate

A range per site, per database or per system, with the work it covers written next to it. The range turns into a fixed quote only after we have seen the inventory and you have answered our questions.

## 4. Your approval

Nothing starts until a named person on your side approves the scope and the price, and grants the access the work needs. You can stop after any step and keep everything produced so far.

## 5. Changes on a separate copy

We work on a copy of your system, never the live one. Upgrades, conversions and fixes are made and run there first.

## 6. Checking

Before and after are compared field by field: pages and their text, row counts, column types, constraints, mailboxes and folders, file properties. Anything that does not match goes on a list with the reason, and you decide what happens to it.

## 7. Cutover

The switch happens at a time you choose, with the old system still able to take traffic back. DNS records, their timings and a tested restore are written down beforehand, so reversing the move is a known set of steps.

## 8. Handover or ongoing care

You get the notes, the comparison and the access back. If you want us to keep looking after the new system, that is a separate agreement priced on its own.

## What we will not do

- Log in to any system you have not given us access to in writing.
- Switch over without a named person's sign-off and a tested way back.
- Copy or reuse code from another project before checking its license.
- Promise a move with no downtime, or a fixed price before we have seen the inventory.
