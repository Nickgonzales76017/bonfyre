# CAD, BIM and design files

For design, engineering and facilities teams moving drawings and building models between tools or file versions, such as IFC2X3 to IFC4.

## What moves

Models and drawings, their layers and properties, linked files, and the naming your team uses to find them.

## What we check first

- The source formats and schema versions are named.
- You provide a representative sample model under your authorization.
- What must stay editable after the move, such as walls, openings and properties, is written down.

## How the result is checked

The sample is converted first. Geometry and properties are compared between the original and the converted file, and anything that became a dead shape or lost a property is listed before the full set moves.
