history.csv and september.csv are the first 6,000 and the next 1,500 lines of
DoDataThings/us-bank-transaction-categories-v2 (MIT licence), a public
synthetic set built to look like US bank statement lines, with its 17
categories. They are nobody's real accounts. september.csv keeps its Category
column so `bonfyre books` can say how many lines it got right.

sample-bank.csv and schedule-c-chart.txt are a small business month written
by hand against the expense lines of IRS Schedule C, for trying books with no
history: every line then goes to the local model and is held for review.
