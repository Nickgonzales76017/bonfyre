# The expense lines of IRS Schedule C (Form 1040), plus income and transfers.
Sales income: Payments received from customers for goods or services
Advertising: Ads, listings, marketing services, promotion
Car and truck expenses: Fuel, parking, tolls, vehicle repairs and rideshare for business driving
Commissions and fees: Bank fees, card processing fees, platform and marketplace fees
Contract labor: Freelancers and contractors who are not employees
Insurance: Business insurance premiums
Interest: Interest on business loans and credit cards
Legal and professional services: Lawyers, accountants, registered agents, filing services
Office expense: Software subscriptions, postage, small office purchases
Rent or lease: Rent for office, storage or equipment
Repairs and maintenance: Repairs to equipment and premises
Supplies: Materials and supplies used in the work
Taxes and licenses: State and local taxes, permits, licences
Travel: Airfare, hotels and transport while travelling away from home
Meals: Business meals
Utilities: Electricity, gas, water, internet, phone
Wages: Payroll to employees
Owner draw or transfer: Transfers between the owner's accounts, owner withdrawals
