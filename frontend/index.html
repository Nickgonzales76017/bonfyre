    How Bonfyre Is Put Together

    Audio File
  ↓  bonfyre-ingest        Intake + validation
  ↓  bonfyre-media-prep    Normalize (16kHz mono)
  ↓  bonfyre-hash          SHA-256 content addressing
  ↓  bonfyre-transcribe    Speech → text (Whisper)
  ↓  bonfyre-transcript-clean  Remove filler
  ↓  bonfyre-paragraph     Structure paragraphs
  ↓  bonfyre-brief         Summary + action items
  ↓  bonfyre-proof         Quality scoring
  ↓  bonfyre-offer         Pricing + outreach copy
  ↓  bonfyre-pack          Assemble deliverable
  →  Delivered Offer ($18, same-day)

Infrastructure: bonfyre-cms, bonfyre-api, bonfyre-gate, bonfyre-index, bonfyre-graph
Orchestration:  bonfyre-pipeline, bonfyre-queue, bonfyre-sync, bonfyre-stitch
Value:          bonfyre-offer, bonfyre-meter, bonfyre-ledger, bonfyre-finance, bonfyre-outreach, bonfyre-pay
Auth:           bonfyre-auth (signup/login/sessions → gate keys)
