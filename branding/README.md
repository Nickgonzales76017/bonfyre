# Aurekai — brand system

**Built on software. Driven by Aurekai.**

**Business:** Aurekai develops, improves and operates digital businesses and the products that support them. **Technology:** Bonfÿre is the shared native engine. The goal is distinctive businesses, not identical sites carrying another provider's logo.

## Four coordinated lines

| Use | Copy | Application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness / LinkedIn | Every brand is built on software. The ones everyone talks about are built on Aurekai. | Bold campaign language, not a customer roster |
| Hero / deck | Every brand runs on software. The ones that lead run on Aurekai. | Aspirational positioning for Aurekai-owned pages |
| Proposal / introduction | Software runs every business. Aurekai powers the ones people remember. | Combine with actual client scope, examples and evidence |
| Footer / card | Built on software. Driven by Aurekai. | Consistent Aurekai-owned signature |

The two bold brand claims are *campaign positioning*, not evidence that named market leaders already use Aurekai. Do not add implied customer logos, paid-client results, market share, certifications, testimonials or revenue claims. Prefer actual public working experiences with clear demonstration limits.

## Four public entrances

| Destination | Editorial heading | Concrete context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work — aurekai.work | Build the business people remember. | CRM, operations, opportunities and financial workflows |
| Life — aurekai.life | Make your corner of the internet your own. | People, profiles, collections and communities |
| Live — aurekai.live | Give your work more places to go. | Creators, recordings, publications and channels |
| Shop — aurekai.shop | Build the business behind every product. | Catalogues, sourcing, inventories, services and offers |

The four destinations must not use one recycled hero. Use a single visual family and signature while preserving distinct tasks, destinations, data and access. **Do not project this copy onto customer-managed, creator-managed, or independent brand sites.** Native publication, CMS records and social-platform profiles require their own destination-owned review and readback.

## Business and technology

- Aurekai: the operating company, products, clients, projects, offers and public experiences.
- Bonfÿre: native system architecture, identity and addresses, evidence, execution, finance, media, applications and shared business logic.
- Public examples: demonstrates a real action only when an observable test or artifact exists. Do not call a sample a client delivery.
- Data/identity: source ownership and account-level permissions remain distinct even where software is shared.
- Open source: do not imply the PolyForm-licensed runtime is permissively licensed.

## Visual and editorial rules

Use professional editorial hierarchy: one clear headline, one specific explanation, product evidence and a direct action. Keep technical terms in the technical guide, not above the fold. Prefer captured working product screenshots, credited photography and diagrams with demonstrable data. No fabricated company/customer imagery, generic AI "brain", sci-fi ornament or unreadable microcopy. Keep mobile line lengths and CTA targets usable.

## Rollout

The repository controls its own Pages and Work Samples authored sources; the source-owned `branding/voice.mjs` is the text contract. `scripts/tests/aurekai-brand.test.mjs` checks critical placements. The runtime four-apex showcase has separate source and activation; a GitHub change alone is not a live four-domain deployment. Social accounts, Google Business, YouTube and LinkedIn are *prepared copy*, not posted until separately reviewed and admitted.
