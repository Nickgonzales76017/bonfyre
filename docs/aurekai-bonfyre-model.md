# Aurekai and Bonfÿre

**Aurekai develops and operates digital products. Bonfÿre is the native platform that runs and connects them.**

AI has changed the economics of building software. A small group equipped with agents can produce applications, integrations and technical systems at a rate that conventional planning and departmental coordination struggle to match. The bottleneck has shifted to architecture, institutional knowledge, integration, operating decisions and distribution.

Aurekai treats engineering capacity, AI computation, reusable software and shared infrastructure as **productive capital**. Bonfÿre turns each project into additional capacity for the rest of the business.

## Aurekai — the operating business

| Division | Products and activity | Public destination |
|---|---|---|
| **Work** | Organizations, jobs, services, relationships, CRM, operations, finance and business applications | [aurekai.work](https://aurekai.work) |
| **Life** | People, profiles, organizations, communities, publishing, collections and interests | [aurekai.life](https://aurekai.life) |
| **Live** | Creators, channels, media, recording, narration and distribution | [aurekai.live](https://aurekai.live) |
| **Shop** | Products, catalogues, offers, price observations, sourcing, commerce and spatial planning | [aurekai.shop](https://aurekai.shop) |

Across these divisions, Aurekai develops products, operates services, connects participants, discovers opportunities, integrates external systems and builds commercial programs.

The **digital private equity** analogy captures its investment mechanism: developer hours, software, compute, shared distribution and operating capability can grow the value of a portfolio of digital assets and projects. Ownership, licensing, partnerships, services and revenue sharing are defined by the actual relationship.

## Bonfÿre — the technology platform

Bonfÿre provides the common systems that individual Aurekai products and organizations can compose.

| System | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Matrix & identity** | People, organizations, subjects, relationships, locations, addresses, source identities and publication presences |
| **Sources & data** | Source Library, external estate, CMS, indexing, embeddings, SQL, analytics, warehouse, structured records and provenance |
| **Business applications** | Frappe, ERPNext, HRMS, CRM, Helpdesk, LMS, Wiki, Drive and Insights grammars projected into native application surfaces |
| **Work & automation** | WorkGraph, schedules, dependencies, capability programs, native execution, agents, review and resource allocation |
| **Model Commons** | Local model runtimes, inference, quantization, memory, retrieval, speech, evaluation and compute backends |
| **Creator & web systems** | Media processing, narration, publishing, channels, PageComposition/SurfaceIR, account pages and public delivery |
| **Markets & spatial** | Catalogue, procurement, product configuration, offers, pricing, 2D/3D planning and commerce |
| **Finance & operations** | Agreements, billing accounts, costs, payments, accounting, capacity and service lifecycle |
| **Connectivity** | Installed capabilities, external accounts, credentials, APIs, MCP, transport and destination-owned integrations |

The implementation is native-first, with C/Zig components and typed contracts, plus browser, SQL and model backends where each is useful. Models and agents are execution resources in the larger platform.

## How an opportunity becomes a product

| Stage | Activity | Reusable result |
|---|---|---|
| **Discover** | Find a market, customer need, company, publication, source or business process | Indexed source or opportunity |
| **Design** | Define users, workflow, application shape, domain data and integration needs | Application and capability contracts |
| **Build** | Compose existing systems and implement what is missing | Native components, app surfaces and data models |
| **Operate** | Connect accounts, publish experiences, run programs and support the product | Working service, content, relationships and usage |
| **Improve** | Measure outcomes, fix gaps and expand shared capability | Better products and a stronger common platform |

This enables a portfolio to develop faster without making every new business recreate its own authentication, data system, editor, CRM, financial tools, AI infrastructure or integration layer.
