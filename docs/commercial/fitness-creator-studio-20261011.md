# Aurekai Fitness Creator Studio

## Product concept

A white-label fitness creator business, not a subscription skin on a workout app. Combine an openGym-class personal training experience, Patreon-like creator/patron relationships, Paddle-like subscription and finance operations, and Aurekai's media, customer service, work, governance and partner runtime.

Users see the creator's brand: storefront, workouts, community, booking, messaging and member purchases. The creator sees a business: programs, publishing, membership terms, member requests, staff assignments, coaching delivery, revenue, payouts, analytics and accountant exports. Members remain independent actors; their workout history is not the creator's inventory.

### The relationship, rather than a sub-user silo

An individual can support several creators, use a shared training history on their own devices, pay for a one-off call with one coach and subscribe to the classes of another. Do not create multiple copies of that actor as subordinate user records under each creator.

The independent coordinates:

- **Actor**: actual person, signed in and able to act in several institutions.
- **Creator institution**: independently branded operator with public site, offers, products, team and legal merchant identity.
- **Support relationship**: follows, supports or subscribes to one creator. Free follower and paying patron both count as audience relationships; status is not payment evidence.
- **Subscription/term**: recurring agreement with provider-backed collection and term changes.
- **Entitlement**: access to specific media, program, member space, included calls or service credits as agreed, scoped to creator and term.
- **Training record**: member-owned local workout data, distinct from membership relationship and commercial billing.
- **Coach/client relationship**: named professional assigned to a particular client, with independently time-bounded consent for specified training data.
- **Creator team**: coaches, content editors, moderators, bookkeepers, support agents and business owners with bounded actions. A role assignment never implicitly opens health or financial data.
- **Dependent / seat**: optional person/seat paid for by another actor under a separate relationship. It is not a shortcut around consent, age or authority.

Do not collapse follower, customer, patron, athlete, coach, trainer-of-record, employee, accountant, merchant and payer into one membership flag. One actor can occupy multiple roles in different creator institutions.

## Customer experience

The creator storefront has programs, video, community, upcoming live classes, supporters and membership tiers. The member app has Today, Train, Programs, Activity, Community, Requests, Bookings and Account. Training records can be used offline and carried to another compatible tracking app by the member.

### Product catalogue

| Offer shape | Purchase/access result | Delivery owner |
|---|---|---|
| Follow free | Public content and selected creator updates | Channel / community |
| Supporter tier | Members-only posts, voting, Q&A or creator extras | CMS/Channel + scoped entitlement |
| Program tier | Versioned fitness programs, routines, demonstrations and challenges | Training app + media/catalog + entitlement |
| Group coaching | Group workspace, class schedule, capped check-ins, recurring or session pack | Scheduling, team assignment, consultation work |
| Individual coaching | Named coach, intake, approved plan, included messages/calls, private progress review | Coach/client consent, WorkGraph, reservation, media |
| One-off product | Challenge, video course, class pass, custom workout, consultation call | Offer, checkout provider, delivery state |
| Creator collaboration | Co-produced program, guest class, sponsorship, affiliate promotion | Work, rights/credits, approved commercial splits |

Tier inclusion describes a benefit; it is not a promise of infinite labor. A creator can bundle a finite number of calls, questions, check-ins and program revisions with service caps, response times and carryover terms.

## Creator Studio

**Today:** upcoming calls, messages needing answers, member help requests, scheduled content, unfinished programs, payment failures and recently completed work.

**Audience:** followers, patrons, paid clients, group cohorts, invitation sources, cancellations, current permissions and engagement. Search only the creator's own audience.

**Programs & Media:** exercise/program library; build from routines, source media and instructor notes; segment/transcribe demonstrations; edit, version, publish and withdraw to selected tiers. Keep creator-owned rights separate from openGym's third-party asset licensing.

**Requests:** members ask for a technique explanation, an equipment-specific routine, an accessible alternative, a topic, a product or support; creator sees price/tier, capacity, votes, revenue opportunity and commitments. Replies and content proposals have statuses.

**Calls:** bookings for consultation, 1:1 coaching, form review, support, group office hours and live classes. Slot hold, confirmation, video provider link, attendance, consent to record, follow-up material and completion are separate facts. An included call credit is spent when its agreed service occurs under the declared policy, not when a browser shows a booking button.

**Community:** posts, cohort challenges, trainer Q&A, program corrections, permissions, moderators and member feedback. A subscriber may reply publicly without disclosing personal training logs.

**Money:** subscriber terms, product sales, recognized invoices, received payment/settlement, failed payment, refunds, fees, split obligations, payouts and program contribution margin. Legal seller and payment provider are explicit.

**Analytics:** follower-to-member conversion, member retention/churn, ARPU, recurring revenue, program sell-through, content-to-purchase attribution, support queue aging, coaching utilization, delivered service per tier and retention by program/cohort. Training outcomes are aggregated only where the member grants access; view/watch or workout records do not by themselves establish an improved health outcome.

**Accounting & Taxes:** period-based earnings reconciliation and accountant-ready exports without inventing tax forms or calling estimates payments.

## The killer interaction: requested content becomes a business asset

1. A paying member asks for a beginner kettlebell program with limited equipment or proposes a new mobility series.
2. Creator receives a scoped request, can quote it, answer privately, accept it as work, or invite members to support/vote for it.
3. Once accepted, WorkGraph holds the assignment and delivery commitment. Existing media and text organs help prepare a lesson, clips, captions and routine. The creator reviews changes before publishing.
4. The approved routine/program is versioned and delivered to its entitled audience. A member imports or opens it through the workout experience without granting the creator unlimited access to their personal records.
5. The members' own training log records sets, dates and progress on their device/instance. Member-chosen consent can expose a limited coach view or only anonymized aggregate engagement.
6. Requests, participation, completion, feedback and financial outcomes flow into the creator's studio. The creator can produce a revised program, offer a workshop or schedule a call.
7. A guest trainer may produce part of the program under a scoped contributor agreement and explicit payment split. An agreement and a payable are not proof that funds were sent.

This closes the loop from demand -> work -> media/program -> member experience -> evidence -> earnings -> next product. The output can be reused across gym memberships, online instructors, physical classes and independent creators.

### Support/call triage

A creator and team should not have one unbounded DM inbox. Every incoming request names its class and entitlement:

- Public content request or poll.
- Member technique question.
- Private support ticket about app/account/billing.
- Paid consultation or included coaching session.
- Account cancellation, refund or dispute, routed to the payment/legal seller.
- Private program review that requires member consent.

Support agents may view billing/support metadata but not training history. Assigned coaches can access only the consented client data. Editors can publish approved material but not issue refunds. Accountants can export money data but not health metrics. Platform operators may diagnose runtime faults without inheriting all these powers.

Requests can become a public FAQ/video, private reply, booked call, work item, paid project or declined request, each with its own terminal state. An unanswered suggestion is not a commitment.

## Finance and taxation

Each financial occurrence has a creator institution, the legally responsible seller, purchaser, product/service classification, price/currency, taxes charged by responsible party, provider, invoice/charge ID, refund/dispute relation, fee, payout recipient and settlement reference.

Separate these in every dashboard and export:

- **Gross sales**: charged amounts before fees and adjustments.
- **Tax collected**: by whom and for what jurisdiction/product, not automatically creator revenue.
- **Refunds, disputes and credits**: related original charges and adjustments.
- **Processor/platform fees**: distinct expense or contra-revenue according to actual contractual treatment.
- **Creator net proceeds**: recognized amount after mapped deductions.
- **Funds received**: bank/payout settlement, reconciled against provider statements.
- **Team/provider payables**: owed share vs paid share, with recipient identity.

Per-day/month/quarter/year reports, product and tier rollups, multi-currency original + reporting-currency columns, earnings/invoice/payment/payout/fee/refund/expense CSVs, accountant export, and downloadable audit bundle with source receipts. A Stripe Connect integration can provide payer/payee onboarding, provider payout reports and US tax-form services where configured. No local estimate is a filed 1099, tax remittance or proof of settled payout. Distinguish US 1099-K gross reporting from creator business net income, and retain the payment provider's forms as provider-origin evidence.

### Billing provider selection

Borrow Paddle's product grammar, not its universal suitability. Paddle's AUP excludes standalone coaching/human services, community access/crowdfunding and some health/weight-loss or muscle-building advice categories. Select a supported PSP/marketplace provider for personal coaching, events and services, with the creator/merchant of record explicitly modeled. MoR options may be appropriate only for separately eligible software/digital products and properly approved merchant relationships.

Billing software and branded checkout can be shared; the lawful seller, tax party, processor and payout obligations do not become shared just because sites run on the same Bonfÿre estate.

## Upstream openGym integration

Current openGym repository: https://github.com/DuarteSantos8/openGym

- Mature personal app: routines, exercise catalogue, guided sessions, rest timers, progress/history/PRs, weight tracking, progress images, offline/PWA/device sync, passkeys, import/export and optional AI coach/MCP. Its training and progression functions live under frontend/src/lib and the native API is documented at api/openapi.yaml.
- Model: per-user state in JSON files and account state, with signed session, revision and field-stamp merge. It does not currently constitute a creator billing, payout, audience or team-workflow backend.
- Roadmap: trainer/student read access and plan writes are currently scheduled for a later release, not proof of a running coaching system. Do not pretend those routes exist.
- AGPLv3: when modifying and network-serving openGym itself, satisfy its source availability/copying terms; assess linking and integration with legal review. A separate compatible upstream instance/adapter may offer a cleaner licensing and ownership boundary than forking it into a proprietary platform, but the mere fact of a process boundary does not settle all license questions.
- Exercise media: Gym visual assets in openGym are separately licensed and cannot be repackaged into a commercial white-label application without rights from the media owner. Use independently licensed media or contributor-supplied assets with valid terms. Do not use upstream restricted media for AI training or generation.

Implementation direction (Nick, October 11): rewrite the training experience as an independently adoptable Bonfÿre library and app. Use openGym's published behavior and interchange contract to track coverage. Own the interaction design, branding, storage and model controls. Keep the creator/team financial and operational surfaces on Bonfÿre's existing owners.

Style controls cover typography, color, density, navigation, exercise presentation and customer assets. Each creator can choose its presentation without changing a member's workout history. WordPress, Frappe and other bridges have separately configured endpoints, mappings and permitted actions. Model settings distinguish local and hosted providers, model identity, allowed data and review of proposed program changes. Saving a connection or selecting a model does not itself transfer training records or execute a proposal.

Member-owned history has one training owner, portable exports and explicit import review. Creator relationships refer to that member; they do not create another workout ledger. Program versions can be adopted into the member's app, while progress sharing requires the member's separate consent.

### Phone and watch connections

The **Devices** screen supports Bluetooth heart-rate sensors, reviewed TCX workout files and calendar handoff. Pairing opens the browser's device chooser for the standard Heart Rate Service. The app displays fresh readings, clears them on loss of contact or connection, and records them only when the member turns on **Save readings with my workouts**. Recording pauses with the workout and retains a reading at most once every five seconds. Saved sessions show the average, peak and a trace of the recorded samples. This is a sample average, not a time-weighted physiological measure.

A TCX file opens a review of workout dates, active time, distance and available average heart rate. Adding it preserves existing training and refuses repeat imports of the same normalized activity. Watch activities remain separate from strength sets and do not complete planned sessions. GPS tracks and device notes are omitted. Both imported activities and saved sensor readings use the existing member store, work offline and travel in the training export.

An active plan can open the phone share sheet with an iCalendar file, or download that file where sharing is unavailable. Upcoming sessions become all-day events with stable identities. Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or another chosen calendar owns import and alerts. This is a snapshot, so later training-plan edits do not update the calendar copy.

Apple Watch and Wear OS have equal priority. The native work remains ahead of us:

| Show live session state on the phone | HealthKit session mirroring | Wearable Data Layer messages with acknowledgments |

Completing a set requires a member action with its actual values. Starting another workout or applying an older device state must not overwrite the current session. Health permissions do not grant a coach access to personal records.

## Demo that should lead the website

Two independently branded instructors. Creator A runs strength programming and recurring group coaching; Creator B runs mobility video courses and 1:1 consultations. One member supports both but has one owned fitness history. Creator A hires a second coach for six assigned clients; the second coach cannot see other patrons or financial totals. The same request for a limited-equipment program is received, accepted, produced, published, used and analyzed. A scheduled call is paid (or consumes an included session credit), with actual booking/attendance outcomes. Payout and annual tax exports reconcile actual sandbox provider transactions, while a refund and disputed charge appear separately.

Acceptance:

1. Member can follow several creators without copied identity or cross-creator content leakage.
2. Entitlement is derived from a real subscription term and provider/payment state, not a static role badge.
3. Content purchase and private training-data access remain distinct. Revoking client consent ends trainer access.
4. Call can be requested, scheduled, cancelled, attended and reconciled; only the actual event consumes its declared session credit.
5. Co-coach, editor, moderator, accountant and creator all see different role-specific programs with native authority checks on every effect.
6. Annual accountant export reproduces gross sales, tax, refunds, fees, chargebacks, split payable, payout and net recognized receipts, with identifiable source references, while excluding private workout/weight data.
7. Customer-brand favicon, published metadata and MCP tool surface identify the relevant fitness creator, not Aurekai, on independent customer sites.
8. Native library/workout app remains independently adoptable; no unlicensed third-party exercise animations enter the commercial app.

The differentiation cannot merely be one software product that combines these functions.

Aurekai's distinctive position: **the fitness creator's business and the member's private fitness software are both independent, yet can compose into real business operations across agencies, teams, communities and suppliers**. Add a native operating/economic substrate, personal data portability, domain and payment independence, the ability to close support and coaching work, and usable branded product surfaces.
