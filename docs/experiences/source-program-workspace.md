# Sources, contents and recurring programs

Choose products from retained sources, build recurring selections and review each cycle's dates and costs. Work in this browser or in a personal, organization or household workspace with current access. Saved programs remain proposals; they do not place orders or activate merchant subscriptions.

## Choose where the work belongs

[Open the browser workspace](https://browser-workbench.aurekai-work-samples.pages.dev/) to work in this browser profile. [Open the native workspace setup](https://aurekai.life/packs/setup?pack=experience.source-programs.workspace&context=self) to choose a destination under your Aurekai account.

In native setup, sign in, choose your personal destination or an available organization or household, review the configuration, then install and activate it. The editor opens over the destination's existing CMS records. A second person with current write access can reopen and continue the same program; read-only access does not permit saving changes. Organization membership does not grant access to a separate household.

The page identifies its storage destination. The public browser workspace keeps its own local records. The native workspace does not fall back to local storage if access is denied. Installing it does not copy existing browser proposals into the destination. The existing catalogue and room-draft transfer does not yet transfer recurring programs and their source dependencies.

## Bring in source material

Choose **Import captures**, or drop saved capture files onto the source library. Review new pages, changed versions, material already retained and invalid files. Select what to keep. Those writes commit together; a conflict leaves the batch unapplied.

The importer accepts the existing browser capture format: up to 40 files, 256 KiB per capture and 8 MB in total. It reads supplied files rather than fetching their addresses. Content-identical captures retain their original observation even when a timestamp changes. A changed page remains a separate version.

Search by title, address or selected passage. Opening a source shows its recorded material without attaching it to the program. Check its selection box to attach it. Product selection also attaches the sources needed by the chosen products.

The installed extension can capture a selected browser tab after your click. The public website cannot read another tab or submit native work without the extension and its approved host. Native destination storage is a separate connection; it does not itself authorize browser capture or merchant actions.

## Build recurring product contents

Open **Product contents**. Product cards show retained names, SKU where available, recorded prices, capture dates and source links. Search by name or SKU and filter by retained source. A product recorded as unavailable cannot be added. No statement here establishes current supplier stock.

Choose **Recurring default contents**, add products and change their quantities. **Replace** lets you select another product while retaining the line's quantity. Adding a product already selected increases its quantity rather than creating another identical line.

Choose **Selected product prices** for an itemized estimate, or **Fixed bundle price** to keep the amount entered in the program's agreement. Fixed bundle prices do not change with the selected product prices. Prepaid gifts and try-on programs retain their separate fixed-price agreement and checkout semantics.

Set minimum units, maximum units and the per-product limit under **Bundle quantity rules**. The selection summary shows progress and the cost. Missing exact USD prices, recorded unavailability and unsatisfied quantity rules remain visible. An unresolved itemized amount is not treated as zero.

Amounts describe one member's cycle. Capacity does not multiply the estimate. The delivery allowance is the amount you entered; tax and actual delivery charges are not quoted.

The adjacent impact table shows every preview cycle, its selection and modeled amount. **Use these contents** applies the changes to the open proposal. **Review changes** and **Save reviewed draft** keep that version. Cancel leaves the proposal unchanged. Unconfirmed contents remain available while navigating within the current editor, but are not a saved backup.

## Customize one cycle without changing the recurring selection

Choose **Edit contents** on a cycle card, or select that cycle in the contents workspace. Add an extra product, replace a product or adjust quantities for that cycle. Later cycles using the recurring defaults keep their own selection.

A customized cycle holds an explicit selection. Changing the recurring defaults later does not rewrite that customized cycle. **Use recurring defaults** removes its override. **Reset customized cycles** returns every customized cycle to the current defaults after confirmation.

Undo and redo include applied contents changes. The readable review shows actual product names, quantities and recorded prices for changed cycles, not only a count of changed fields. A source required by the saved program remains attached.

## Plan dates and compare upcoming programs

Open **Cycle planner** to choose one of the existing 36 templates and edit the first date, cadence and agreement assumptions. Use **Skip in proposal** or **Restore cycle** on a card, or select several cycles for a bulk action.

**Reschedule** reviews another date with one-week and original-date shortcuts. It shows the affected months' costs before confirmation. Later cycles keep their dates. The moved card retains its original date, and a skipped cycle remains skipped. Its product selection follows the original cycle identity.

A date cannot collide with or pass a neighboring cycle, and a move is limited to 366 days from the original date. Reset moved dates or customized contents when their original anchors no longer belong to an edited series. A stock-triggered program has no invented delivery calendar. Skipping a prepaid gift delivery does not create a refund.

**Upcoming programs** groups the open proposal and saved programs by date, with a month selector and an option to include skipped cycles. Separate programs on the same date remain separate agreements. The current open version replaces its saved copy in this view, avoiding a duplicate estimate. **Edit this cycle** opens the exact program and cycle; keep any unsaved changes before switching programs.

## Adopt a changed source across selected programs

Retain a later capture of the same source page, then open **Source updates**. Choose the older source and the later capture and select **Review affected programs**.

The review finds saved programs using the old source in their contents. It shows product prices before and after, plus the affected monthly costs. Fixed-price agreements keep their price. Product matching uses the retained SKU and product and offer addresses; it does not assume that the same array position or similar title identifies a product. A missing or ambiguous match requires an explicit replacement. A newly unavailable product or invalid resulting selection cannot be adopted through the batch action.

Select the programs to update. No program is selected automatically. **Update selected saved programs** asks for confirmation, then checks the reviewed program and source revisions and commits the selected changes in one transaction. A stale program or source refuses the whole batch. Reopen the review against the current versions before retrying.

Unselected programs and both captured source bodies stay unchanged. The current editor is not overwritten after a batch update: reopen an updated program before continuing. Native workspaces use the same operation with destination-owned CMS records and current access checks.

## Save, reopen and hand off the result

**Save reviewed draft** writes the program and checks its selected sources in the same storage transaction. A stale tab cannot replace a newer saved version. **Open full studio** reads the same stored recurring programs and provides a return link to the exact program for contents editing.

**Download readable proposal** and **Print proposal** include terms, selected products, dates, costs and source references. The separate interoperable export remains available for existing integrations. These exports do not create an approval, merchant connection, stock reservation, invoice or charge.

The native source-library interface currently keeps sources rather than offering removal. In the browser edition, removal refuses a source referenced by a saved program. Browser storage can be cleared by its owner; readable exports are not a full source-library backup. Automatic provider synchronization, merchant execution and recurring-program transfer between browser and native destinations remain separate work.
