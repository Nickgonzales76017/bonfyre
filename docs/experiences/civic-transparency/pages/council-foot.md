## Your town is not here

Many cities publish their agendas on a service that can be read by machine, but not all of them answer every day, and some keep agendas in other systems. If your town is missing:

1. Search your city's agenda site for "license plate", "LPR" and the camera company's name.
2. Look for a purchasing or consent item. Camera contracts often pass without discussion as part of a consent agenda.
3. Send what you find through the [contact page](/contact), or skip straight to [asking your city](/ask) for the contract.
