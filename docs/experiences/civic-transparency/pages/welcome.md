North Texas towns have put up thousands of license plate readers, and most residents learn about them from a pole on the corner. This site gathers what the public record says about them. Cameras counts the readers volunteers have mapped in Dallas, Tarrant, Collin and Denton counties, town by town. Council votes lists the contracts and briefings in city agendas. Ask your city has a ready letter for the city's records. Search logs explains what a city's search records can show without exposing anyone. Corrections are posted here, dated, as they are made.
