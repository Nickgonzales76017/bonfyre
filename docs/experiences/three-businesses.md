# Three businesses. One software foundation.

Three live websites, each with its own name, look, pages and offers, all run from one Bonfÿre installation. The businesses are made up; the sites are real and open to visit. Aurekai delivers the software, and the finished site belongs to the business.

## Under the customer experience

Each business has its own address, published content and assistant endpoint. Underneath, all three use the same parts.

| Customer sees | Bonfÿre provides | Operator uses |
|---|---|---|
| Pages, offers and answers | CMS, WebAddress, presentation templates | `bonfyre site page`, `offer`, `show` |
| Questions and booking requests | Site MCP, scoped credentials and lead records | `bonfyre site inbox` |
| Confirmed work | Commitments, terms and operator actions | `bonfyre site accept` |
| Invoices and settlement records | Meter, Pay, Finance and source-linked books | `bonfyre site paid`, Finance books |
| Content from other systems | Bridge and import/export tools | `bonfyre bridge`, `site import`, `site export` |
| Search and assistant access | Site search, scoped MCP tools | `bonfyre site search`, `mcp`, `usage` |

The assistant route is not the product. Each site works in a regular browser, with MCP as another permitted interface over the same records.

## Reuse without blending customers together

A partner can configure the same parts for a second business without copying the first business's identity, enquiries, financial history or permissions. A new website is not a new data platform.

Each site's look is one presentation template: colours, typefaces, the shape of the front page and the menu. The three templates used here are in `docs/experiences/presentations`. Images and a favicon are still added per customer.
