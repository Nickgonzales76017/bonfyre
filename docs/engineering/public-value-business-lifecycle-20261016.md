# Public value, business configuration and ongoing operations

Research and implementation requirements, October 16, 2026. This extends `cmd/BonfyreExperienceStage/FOLD-BACK.md` and the existing portfolio. It is not an implemented product, new registry, published user guide or completion receipt.

## Required product outcome

A stranger can use Aurekai without an account or knowledge of Work Samples. From the normal public site they can obtain a useful result, keep working, establish their own identity, create a new organization or household, configure its public and operational experience, invite people, connect resources, receive customer activity and payments, fulfill work, and change how the organization operates. Entering through a demo is optional. Existing demonstration businesses must be reproducible from empty customer state through the same product controls available to a new customer.

There are two independent starting paths: public utility followed by explicit continuation, and direct creation of a new organization without first viewing a demo. Passing one does not establish the other. Public infrastructure and released capability definitions may already exist; customer organizations, members, products, provider connections and accepted transactions may not be pre-seeded to make the test pass.

The public side and operating side must form a repeatable cycle: useful public material -> participation or customer demand -> organizational work -> delivered outcome -> permitted public distribution and further participation. A suite of independently impressive editors does not close this cycle.

## Public and authenticated experience

The landing must offer real current material and useful actions: read, watch, listen, search, filter, compare, inspect a product, explore an event, try a permitted creator tool, or begin a plan. Site visitors should not need to visit Work Samples to discover that Aurekai can do work. Public source material must retain its attribution and freshness; third-party observations must not impersonate participating businesses or imply live inventory.

Use contextual account requests. Saving across devices, collaborating, posting, joining, requesting service and operating a business can ask for the required identity at that moment. Preserve the selected object, draft, return path and intended action. Anonymous exploration may stay local; it must not covertly upload files, start paid jobs, publish or join marketing lists.

The signed-in personal home should add saved work, followed subjects, messages, upcoming commitments and a clear way into organizations without replacing discovery with a feature dashboard. An organization should have its public profile/site, customer-facing offerings and private operational views. A profile's public claims and presentation never confer control over a business or venue.

Creating a workspace for a new organization and claiming a pre-existing observed business are separate actions. The former creates new internal ownership; the latter requires the existing identity/claim owner to establish control. A supplier, client, community member, contractor, household member and employee need different relationships, not a single all-purpose member flag.

## Business configuration, not single-business cloning

Keep specialist algorithms and interaction depth. Move instance-specific names, example customers, sources, assets, destinations and policy choices out of editor code. Separate sample data from defaults. New installations must start empty unless the user explicitly chooses sample or reusable content. A template cannot copy private customer records, historic approvals, members, credentials or money.

A released business configuration must compose participants and roles; the offered object or outcome; request/booking/purchase/subscription/negotiation behavior; pricing and funding; capacity and schedule; fulfillment and acceptance; disclosure and publication; resource/provider bindings; and day-two controls. Valid combinations are checked by the existing owners and compilers. Unsupported combinations name the missing capability rather than becoming a generic editable status field.

Existing families to exercise include physical sales, second-hand disposition, services and quoted work, appointments/rentals, recurring membership, recurring contents/group purchasing, media/licensing, sponsorship/creator work, civic/public-good programs, organizations/cooperatives and internal operational tools. These are test families across the retained portfolio, not a replacement classification or a claim of complete coverage.

The business creator should configure a real offering and see customer, participant and operator views of the same objects. Task-specific setup asks only for choices that affect the selected activity. Deep settings remain available later. A long explanation page, flat collection of buttons or endless wizard is not sufficient.

## First-time organization path

Start in a fresh browser at a normal Aurekai apex. Explore without signing in, or choose to start a business directly. Create an account and organization through product UI. Select what the organization offers and how its customers participate. Build an initial offering from empty state; choose public appearance, audience, price/funding and fulfillment rules. Bind required data/resources, configure supported payment onboarding, invite a teammate and preview the different roles.

Configuration must actually create or bind the necessary domain records, jobs, views, routes and application components. Resume interrupted setup without duplicates. A provider review or unavailable resource may keep a dependent capability pending while unrelated browsing, authoring and free operations remain usable. The setup completion view must explain the remaining task and provide its real control.

A second browser representing an unrelated customer discovers the released offering through public navigation or an ordinary shared link, participates or buys, receives the expected confirmation and delivered value, and can return for support. The organization sees the same customer work, its money state, assigned work and fulfillment outcome. The first account can remain a consumer of other organizations at the same time.

## SHOUTOUT acceptance activity

Use SHOUTOUT as an out-of-sample productization test for the current editor-focused work, not as a new hard-coded promotional demo. Preserve its original venue, sponsor, creator, audience, time/place, live media and economic relationships.

A visitor finds a public event, creator or permitted offer and can inspect or consume it without operating an organization. A new venue or campaign organization configures a sponsorship/creator activation: objective, place/time or remote scope, deliverables, capacity, budget, rights, approval and payment conditions. A sponsor and creator have their own identities and views; the organization can invite staff without lending them every financial power.

A creator discovers eligible work, reviews the brief and compensation, accepts or proposes terms, and delivers the agreed material. The editor works on the actual assignment. The buyer reviews a specific delivery, requests a revision or accepts it, and the payment owner records the corresponding payment/payout outcome. Released media and offers reach the permitted profile, channel, feed or event views and can support a later campaign.

Include a creator decline, a replacement, a late or rejected delivery, partial completion, withdrawal and the agreed refund/cancellation path. Attribution records identify what was observed and must not treat views, scans or clicks as guaranteed sales. Do not execute live campaigns, contact creators, pay or collect precise visitor location for this research pass.

## Resources and ongoing operations

Resource settings cover data sources, payment accounts, media delivery, compute/models, storage, staff, physical equipment, venues and suppliers where the relevant business uses them. A replacement preview must show compatibility, capacity, geographic/data restrictions, cost basis, pending work, customer impact and migration requirements. Unknown price or capacity is not zero or unlimited.

An approved switch normally routes new work to the replacement and preserves existing assignments, reservations, payment references, refunds and historical artifacts until their contracts permit migration. Test, migrate, drain and revoke old access deliberately. A payment account switch does not port stored payment credentials automatically; a media host switch does not erase existing rights or published addresses. Reuse ProviderGraph, ServiceLifecycle, resource and financial owners rather than an editor-specific endpoint selector.

Day-two controls include orders/requests, participants, assignments, conversations, reminders, capacity, customer access, invoices/payment outcomes, refunds, disputes, support, public moderation and relevant analytics. These are domain operations over existing owners; they must not be reimplemented as a parallel universal workspace database. Personal training/health state stays actor-private even when the studio's programs and operations are shared.

Separate platform subscription fees, customer-to-business payments, creator/provider payouts, procurement spending and internal cost allocation. Their actors and permissions differ. Payment behavior must include delayed confirmation, failure, retries, reconciliation and authorized cancellation/refund. Provider sandbox verification and production payment execution are separate release claims.

Retention remains with RetentionGC. Its needed upload class, active leases, retained historical references, transaction-compatible collection and quota recovery remain part of this refactor. Preserve access-policy refusals; do not create a second collector to bypass them.
