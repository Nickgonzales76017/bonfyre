Whose host is this

A host on this estate is not a deployment. It is an institution in a territory. leaflounge.aurekai.shop is the Leaf Lounge as the market sees it; leaflounge.aurekai.life is the same institution as its own people see it; and theleaflounge.store is that institution again under a domain it owns. Same code, same surfaces, different subject.

A subject, addressed

A surface is a representation; the address is the subject. This estate addresses a subject at /s/<kind>/<id> and its room at /room/<kind>/<id> — the same path on every host, because the address belongs to the subject and not to the territory. Follow one and the host answers you directly.

Four territories, four sets of UX physics

They are projections over one Matrix, not four products — but their physics differ, and building them as one feed with different icons would lose exactly what makes each market leader good at what it does. A verb outside its territory is a category error, so nothing below merges them.

Modes

Every other surface on this estate runs a donor project's own front end. This one does not, and that is the point: Zulip, Pixelfed, Pinry, Mobilizon and Next.js Commerce are each one product with one domain. Nothing in the reference estate is shaped like “one subject seen four ways”, so there is nothing here to borrow honestly.

An address is eligibility, not authority. Resolving a subject on a host tells you that host will answer for it. It does not tell you that you may do anything to it.
