The best source is not the one with the most entries.

A source that files ten thousand headlines illuminates one dimension of the world ten thousand times. A council page that files two hundred items naming money, people, organisations and dates illuminates six. This ranks every source this estate reads by how many dimensions its material actually carries, measured as a share of what it files rather than whether it ever happened once.
