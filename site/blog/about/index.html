The Aurekai editorial desk

The Aurekai Journal is a research and editorial publication about software, culture, economics, evidence, and the public internet. Articles are credited to Aurekai editorial as an organization, rather than assigning an individual author without a confirmed individual byline.

How the essays are made

Editorial writing develops from Aurekai and Bonfÿre research, public source material, technical investigations, and original arguments. AI-assisted drafting and production tools may be used. Each published essay is edited for public reading and linked to its retained source; the Journal distinguishes reported facts, cited work, examples, and proposed ideas. The original botanical artwork and explanatory vector illustrations are generated for this publication, not photographs or records of real events.

Sources, rights, and corrections

References and linked evidence are provided so readers can inspect the basis of an argument. An attributed quotation is not an endorsement by the quoted writer. Some essays interpret outside research, and the source remains under its original publisher's rights and terms. Published GitHub source permits a reader to inspect exactly what the Journal released.

To request a correction or inspect an essay's origins, use the public editorial source directory or the linked editable source on each article. The editorial desk does not imply that a future Aurekai feature has already shipped unless the article provides evidence.
