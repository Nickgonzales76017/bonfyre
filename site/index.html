We build businesses

Aurekai develops and operates applications, publications, communities, marketplaces and services. Some are our own; others belong to the people and organizations using them. Bonfÿre is the technology we build once and use throughout that work.

That includes ordinary business software—CRM, finance, inventory, websites and customer accounts—as well as creators' tools, local models, data systems and agents. The next project can draw on software and knowledge we already developed.

The business

One company, many
kinds of work.

Aurekai can run several different products and still share the people, software, data and operating capacity behind them. That's the thinking behind investing engineering time and reusable software in projects that can help the rest of the business.

AI makes it possible to build new applications quickly. Stanford's 2026 AI Index found that 88% of surveyed organizations used AI in at least one business function in 2025. What takes longer is learning an industry, connecting its systems, dealing with customers and keeping the result running. Aurekai brings that work together. A useful component built for one client might also help a creator, a local business or another product we operate.

Businesses

Existing businesses have a head start.

A company already has customers, records, staff and ways of working. We can modernize its WordPress or PHP site, connect its existing database, add CRM or finance workflows, or put a new customer experience on top of what it already owns.

Connected applications

Information should be
useful in more than one place.

One of the harder parts of running several products is what happens between them. Bonfÿre keeps the source and subject consistent while different applications handle their own work.

A job posting

From a feed to a relationship.

Consider a company posting a new role. Aurekai can retain the original listing, show it in discovery and continue it into account-owned CRM work when an installed app can accept that kind of record. A creator or accessibility workflow could also prepare narration from the same source. Both uses keep a link to the original listing and the company that posted it.

A product catalogue

From a listing to a room plan.

A supplier's product details may be useful in a shop, a procurement comparison and a 3D room design. Aurekai's catalogue and spatial work is being built around those continuations, with the original product, selected options, quantity and pricing carried forward where the receiving application needs them.
