Back in Aurekai, review the destination and each proposed change. An existing, different draft is not selected automatically. Nothing is adopted until you confirm there. A source submission receipt does not make the destination work approved or sent.

What this release connects

The specialized catalogue, 3D product inspection and room editors now have a native destination-owned draft path. Within one organization, an authorized member can reopen the same saved plan through a separately installed room editor. Personal workspaces and households have separate drafts; household access currently requires the recorded steward.

This is not a supplier order, a merchant publication or an account-permission grant. The complete multi-member and two-origin adoption journeys were tested with isolated native accounts and the deployed release; no production test account was created.
