Aurekai / the operating business

We build and run
digital businesses.

Aurekai develops applications, services and online experiences for its own projects and for other organizations. That could mean a company website and its back office, a creator's publication, a place for a community to organize, or a market built around inventory from several suppliers.

Each one has different customers and work to do. They share Bonfÿre, the native software platform we use for accounts, data, finance, media, models, integrations and running the applications themselves.

HOW AUREKAI GROWS

New work builds on work we've already done.

We can build a catalogue for one shop, then use much of the same technology for procurement, room planning or another retailer. The second project benefits from the first; both remain their own businesses.

The company

One company can support many kinds of businesses.

An agency working with several shops, a publisher with contributors, a local service business and a creator with an audience should each have an experience that fits them. They shouldn't all look like the same software product with a new logo.

Aurekai's model is to invest engineering time, software, AI, knowledge and distribution in those operations. Some opportunities are products we build; some involve providing software or services to existing businesses; others may involve sourcing, licensing or other commercial arrangements. We can share the expensive technical work while keeping the business relationships distinct.

EXISTING BUSINESSES

Make their systems useful together.

Keep a business's established customers and information while improving its website, forms, reporting, records or back office. Our work includes WordPress, PHP, database migrations, Frappe applications and browser tools.

Connected work

Different applications can work with the same information.

Here's where a shared software foundation changes what we can build. Each application handles its own work, but source information, company relationships and account permissions can travel between them.

A NEW JOB LISTING

Discovery and CRM

A job posted by a company can appear in discovery and remain linked to that company. An account's CRM might use it to create an opportunity; a media application might turn the posting into a narrated update. We're developing the account-app routing so those continuations use the one retained listing.
