Accountability

Kept honest by people, not surveillance

  • Stewards admit members. Only people a community has accepted can post in it.
  • Posts stay where they were made. Nothing here is copied to your profile or sold on.
  • Disagreements go to the circle. A community's steward settles disputes in its own conversation.
