This 2019 Chevrolet Corvette is a ZR1 coupe that was optioned with over $13k worth of equipment including the 3ZR Premium Equipment Group, an eight-speed automatic gearbox, Carbon Flash 19″ and 20″ 10-spoke wheels, Jet Black Napa leather and sueded microfiber upholstery, and blue stitching. The aforementioned package added the contents of the Memory and Sueded Microfiber-Wrapped Upper Trim packages as well as a Bose surround sound system, a Performance Data and Video Recorder, heated and ventilated GT bucket seats, carbon-fiber interior trim, and front curb-view cameras. The car also has a supercharged 6.2-liter LT5 V8, an electronic limited-slip differential, Shadow Gray Metallic paint,…

The ZR1 features a clear-coated carbon-fiber front splitter and rocker-panel extensions along with a vented carbon-fiber hood and exposed carbon fiber on the cooling vent surrounds and engine cover. This example is finished in Shadow Gray Metallic with a carbon-fiber removable roof panel and rear wing, and further exterior details include xenon headlights, heated side mirrors, and a multi-mode exhaust system with quadruple outlets. A paint-correction service was carried out in 2022, at which time touch-ups were performed and paint-protection film was applied.
