Historical runs should improve the current machine, not survive as parallel machinery.

Long-running autonomous projects tend to accumulate campaign folders, supervisors, copied source trees, transcripts, caches, and one-off control planes. Keeping all of it “just in case” feels safe, but it creates competing sources of truth. Deleting it wholesale is worse because unique evidence and executable capability disappear with the noise.

Bonfÿre uses four explicit fates for historical material:

  • ABSORB unique capability, fixtures, receipts, relationship state, benchmarks, tests, or source-backed observations into a current semantic owner.
  • FREEZE rare historical evidence once, content-addressed and byte-preserved outside hot source paths.
  • DELETE only reproducible outputs, exact duplicates, superseded supervisors, caches, and generated logs—and only after machine-readable deletion proof.
  • BLOCK uncertain material with an explicit reason. Uncertainty is not permission to delete.

Absorb consequences, not campaigns

A recent replay projected Run 1–7 history into current owners without copying an old campaign tree. Durable work consequences went to WorkGraph. Claim relations went to EvidenceGraph. Proven, cooled, and open layers went to ProofFrontier. External state became rights-limited ForeignTwins and Partner Commons observations. Repeated trace patterns became Aurekai PatternGraph candidates.

The boundaries are part of the result:

  • A ForeignTwin grants observation and reference only unless stronger rights are explicitly established.
  • An evidence relation is directional: evidence supporting a claim does not make the claim support the evidence.
  • Observation time and retrieval time stay distinct.
  • Aurekai PatternClusters may propose candidates but cannot prove or promote them automatically.
  • Secret-bearing evidence is redacted before content addressing.

Replay is the proof that absorption is real

The compact absorption fixture is idempotent. Its first projection queues four current consequences; replay queues none again. The same validation checks that eight external observations retain their typed boundaries, that proof-frontier records preserve reheat and invalidation conditions, and that the fixture does not contain a supervisor or campaign implementation.

The frozen Run 6 database also replays into five typed work planes with 241 routable rows. One unknown coordinator target is rejected rather than silently coerced, and economic categories remain separate: qualified asking value does not become realized cash.

Why this changes future work

Once history is projected into current owners, future workers do not need to excavate old run directories or trust narrative summaries. They can query active work, evidence relations, known non-causes, regression fences, partner observations, and candidate patterns directly. An unchanged blocker can cool while another frontier advances.

The result is a smaller and more truthful machine: history remains available as evidence, current semantics have one owner, and deletion decisions are backed by proof rather than chronology or disk pressure.
