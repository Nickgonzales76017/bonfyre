The rule this world runs on

A spatial action is an intent signal, never an automatic authoritative mutation. Every interaction port is classified, and the class is shown before you act:

  • decorative — personal arrangement, never leaves the habitat
  • projection — changes how it is shown, not what it is
  • attachment — proposes a candidate relation, not yet canonical
  • work intent — opens a WorkGraph intent, queued not executed
  • authoritative — cannot execute from the world at all; opens a Review Gate a human must answer

The world is a projection, and a projection does not own the record it projects. Confirming a Review Gate here records the intent — it does not write to capital.db or fabric.db.
