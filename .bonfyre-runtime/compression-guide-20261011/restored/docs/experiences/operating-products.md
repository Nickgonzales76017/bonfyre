## A product has more than one entrance

| For the customer | For the operator | For the developer | For assistants |
|---|---|---|---|
| Branded site or application | Permissions, records, work and finances | Native libraries, CLI, typed contracts and APIs | Scoped MCP tools and structured views |
| An offer or service | A plan and price | Rate and entitlement contract | Approved discovery or action |
| A submitted request | A review and assignment | Execution and source receipts | Verified status |
| The delivered result | Reconciliation and support | Reusable software component | Readback or next action |

A branded UI is only one part of white label. Your clients retain their own institutional identities, authorized users, contracts, business terms and provider relationships.
