# White-label operating products

## The product

Aurekai delivers complete operating applications; Bonfÿre supplies the shared native machinery. An agency can deliver those applications under its own identity, and its customers can use their own domains, interfaces, programs, fees and terms. That is software wholesale rather than a collection of themed websites.

One institutional operation may be presented in a browser, customer portal, operator desk, CLI, library and MCP app. All views refer to the same subject, contract, work, authority, evidence and economic records. The system must not introduce parallel billing, work or identity tables simply to satisfy a new screen.

White label has four independent identity coordinates: the platform owner, partner/operator, branded merchant or institution, and legal seller/fiscal host/payment recipient. The visual brand does not transfer legal seller identity or control of funds.

## Paddle: benchmark the entire sale

Paddle provides checkout, recurring billing, subscriptions, tax and regulatory responsibilities, fraud, customer self-service and recovery as a Merchant of Record. Bonfÿre's existing Meter, Pay, Gate, Economy, Finance, WebAddress, Auth and CommerceChain provide important native machinery, but do not constitute a licensed Merchant of Record, global tax remittance service or complete recurring subscription engine.

The actual CommerceChain stage owner distinguishes product, listing, inventory, availability, offer, quote, cart, checkout, commitment, payment, settlement, fulfilment, delivery, satisfaction and ownership. The canonical Atlas MoneyGraph entry points to archived Python and is not a witnessed standalone native financial system. Do not present a payment reference as a bank settlement or a pricing table as a MoR license.

Lago is a useful upstream/interop reference for subscription, usage, credits, entitlements, invoices and self-hosting. Paddle/Stripe Connect fill different regulated financial roles. Reuse those rails under the existing provider and institution boundary instead of rebuilding risk networks or tax filing.

## The three flagship demonstrations

### 1. Independently branded revenue platform

An independent SaaS company creates monthly and annual plans, bundled usage and a metered overage. A real service execution produces a usage receipt and a priced line item. The same product is seen in customer checkout, merchant back office, native CLI and a scoped MCP app. A processor sandbox event produces an acknowledged payment; a cancellation changes entitlement and subsequent access. The invoice still identifies the correct legal seller.

Test two merchants using one installation: tenant A cannot change tenant B's rate, page, buyer, payment instrument or entitlement. Retry the same usage event, exercise upgrade/downgrade/proration and verify no double charge. Keep receipt, revenue and pending collection distinct.

### 2. Community project to completed local work

A branded PTA, club or fiscal-hosted collective proposes a project and allows members to discuss or vote. A supporter pledges money, a fiscal host acknowledges actual collection, suppliers quote, the appropriate administrator accepts a bid, an authorized provider completes the work, and the community sees progress and permitted finances.

Test one member/one vote, institution-scoped budgets, pledge versus collected funds, expense approval by the required parties and refusal to spend another collective's money. Show the public community page, board desk, fiscal-host desk and supplier portal.

### 3. Incident to field-service settlement

A weather or infrastructure event is linked to a property. Evidence is retained and a customer or adjuster opens a scoped case. Contractors respond with a quotation and availability. The authorized buyer accepts one quote, an assignment is dispatched, the work is observed and accepted, and a real payment receipt reconciles the obligation.

Show a map, case timeline, provider capacity, customer/contractor apps and finance. Deny unaccepted quote dispatch, wrong-customer read, absent time reservation, and interpreting an invoice as collected funds.

## Experience and presentation

Aurekai profile: lead with three complete businesses and their output, not a tile for every low-level command. Bonfÿre developer profile: lead with the shared primitives, native implementation, CLI/library/API/MCP examples and release-bound results.

The documentation is the deep gallery: Revenue, Service, Creator, Commons, Claims, Territory, Trade, Contractor and Civic. Each guide connects customer, operator, developer, partner and assistant views to the same execution identity. Use different visual composition for each vertical; one recolored dashboard is not nine products.

Each completed guide must contain the actual invoked command/API, source and contract generation, a witnessed result, readback, a failed unauthorized attempt, and screenshots at desktop/mobile widths. Docs publication is owned by WebAddress and uses the existing CMS and Fabric owners.

## Commercial distribution

Sell complete solutions, paid native libraries and retained delivery/operation. Allow partner resale and white-label deployments. Charge subscription/seat/usage/delivery and provider pass-through where supported by explicit terms. The platform's shared service costs and resource capacity are counted once; neither another customer's payment nor an unrelated institution's funding is pooled by assumption.

The opportunity is a recursive channel: the software enables a partner's business; their customers transact; those transactions create further demand for shared resources, suppliers and native capabilities. Every extra market benefits from the same operating substrate while preserving domain-specific commitments.
