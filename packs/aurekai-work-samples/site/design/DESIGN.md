## Brand & Style

The brand identity embodies the quiet, bespoke confidence of a private high-jewelry atelier. Designed for an audience of discerning collectors, connoisseurs, and master craftspeople, the interface prioritizes restraint, deliberate spatial pacing, and absolute clarity. The emotional atmosphere is calm, tactile, and permanent—reminiscent of heavy rag paper, hand-cut stone, polished gold, and quiet consultation salons.

The design movement blends **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with understated **Classical Architecture**:
- Heavy use of warm negative space rather than decorative filler.
- Disciplined typographic hierarchy that elevates material specifications and provenance records to artifact status.
- Soft, tangible tactile panels paired with hairline stone borders to preserve clarity without loud visual noise.
- Deliberate exclusion of generic digital flourishes, intense drop shadows, or saturated alerts in favor of muted natural minerals.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from fine jeweler bench materials: uncut tourmaline, polished parchment, hand-worked silver, and aged stone.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas & Backgrounds**:
  - Base Ground: `#FBF9F5` (Warm Ivory)

  - Brand Master: `#1B3B2B` (Deep Forest Green)

  - Display & Key Headings: `#181816` (Deepest Charcoal)

  - Primary Structural Line: `#E3DED4` (Muted Stone)
  - Secondary / Hover Hairline: `#D5CFC3` (Aged Limestone)

  - Added / Created: `#E6ECE8` background with `#2D5A43` foreground (Soft Sage)
  - Modified / Pending: `#F5EFE3` background with `#7A5B28` foreground (Muted Amber)
  - Preserved / Neutral: `#EFECE6` background with `#57544E` foreground (Parchment Gray)

## Typography

Typography establishes tension between the high-contrast luxury serif (`Bodoni Moda`) and the structural, calm precision of the neutral grotesque (`Manrope`).

- **Bodoni Moda**: Reserved for titles, collection labels, provenance markings, and narrative quotes. Rendered with generous line-heights to emphasize editorial breathing room.
- **Manrope**: Applied to all body text, technical gemstone specifications, prices, navigational items, and input controls. Selected for its optical balance, modern geometry, and effortless legibility against light ivory surfaces.
- **Micro-Typography & Labels**: All metadata (`label-sm`, `label-md`) are set uppercase with tracked letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.14em`) to reference traditional hallmarks and archival catalog tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to a classical 12-column proportional grid system designed to simulate archival folio layouts.

### Screen Adaptability
- **Desktop (1200px and up)**: 12-column layout, `margin-lg` (4rem), `gutter-lg` (2.5rem). Maximum container bounds capped at 1440px to ensure intimate compositions.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column layout, `margin` (2rem), `gutter` (1.5rem). Compact two-tier cards collapse into fluid horizontal stacks.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column layout, `margin-sm` (1.25rem), `gutter-sm` (1rem). High-priority information remains center-aligned or aligned left along strict baseline rules.

Vertical spacing utilizes deliberate multiples of `1.25rem` (`space-md`) and `2rem` (`space-lg`), creating generous room around imagery and valuation figures.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained via flat tonal shifts and structural hairlines rather than heavy synthetic drop shadows.

- **Surface Layering**: The primary canvas rests on `#FBF9F5`. Contained units, spec drawers, and ledger listings reside on `#F5F1EA`. Elevated modal sheets use pure `#FFFFFF` with an ultra-subtle translucent border.
- **Border-First Definition**: Containers and panels rely on a crisp 1px border (`#E3DED4`). When grouped together, panels snap side-by-side using shared single borders to echo architectonic grid structures.
- **Ambient Floor Shadows**: Floating dropdowns and dialog overlays adopt a soft, warm feather shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(27, 59, 43, 0.04), 0 2px 6px rgba(34, 34, 32, 0.03)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces tailored, compact roundedness that honors traditional gem faceting and framed bevels:
- **Base Components**: Input fields, buttons, and status chips apply compact corners (`0.25rem` / `4px`), keeping them crisp and architectural.
- **Panels & Display Cards**: Surfaces, modals, and inventory sheets use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) to soften the framing without feeling playful.
- **Full Circles**: Strictly reserved for gemstone color swatches, carat weight indicators, and user monograms.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep Forest Green background (`#1B3B2B`), crisp warm ivory text (`#FBF9F5`), 1px solid `#1B3B2B`. Hover shifts to `#244C38`. Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`.
- **Secondary / Atelier Outline**: Transparent ground, 1px border `#D5CFC3`, text `#181816`. Hover shifts background to `#F5F1EA` with border `#1B3B2B`.
- **Tertiary / Link**: No border, underlined with 1px stone stroke (`#E3DED4`) offset by 4px, font weight medium, tracking `0.04em`.

### Cards & Panels
- **Composition**: Ground `#F5F1EA`, 1px border `#E3DED4`, `rounded-lg` geometry. Internal padding: `space-md` or `space-lg`.
- **Imagery Containers**: Subdued off-white ground (`#EFE9DF`) to elevate jewelry assets with neutral temperature.

### Chips & Diff Status Indicators
- **Added / Certified**: Ground `#E6ECE8`, border 1px solid `#CAD6CE`, text `#2D5A43`, letter spacing `0.08em`, uppercase.
- **Modified / Reserved**: Ground `#F5EFE3`, border 1px solid `#DFD3BD`, text `#7A5B28`, uppercase.
- **Specification Chips**: Ground `#FBF9F5`, border 1px solid `#E3DED4`, text `#222220`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Flat background `#FBF9F5`, border 1px solid `#E3DED4`, placeholder `#6A6862`. On focus: border becomes `#1B3B2B` with zero outer glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 16px square/circle, border 1px solid `#D5CFC3`. Selected state: `#1B3B2B` fill with `#FBF9F5` custom check glyph.

### Lists & Ledger Rows
- Segmented with hairline horizontal dividers (`#E3DED4`). Hover effect introduces a seamless background wash of `#F5F1EA` without changing shape or height.
