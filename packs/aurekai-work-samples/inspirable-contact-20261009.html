Frequently asked questions

What industries do you specialize in?

+

Small and mid-size businesses across professional services, medical, legal, restaurants, real estate, and local retail. We also work with government agencies, financial institutions, tribal nations, and nonprofits — organizations that need secure, compliant, and scalable web infrastructure.

What's included in a care plan?

+

24/7 security monitoring, malware scanning and removal, firewall configuration, automated security patching, SSL management, encrypted backups every 5 hours, and monthly performance reports on SOC 2 Type II certified infrastructure through our data center partner. We also maintain disaster recovery plans with documented RTO/RPO targets and quarterly restore drills — available on request. We don't publicly disclose the full details of our security stack, because doing so would be a security issue in itself. Starting at $89.99/mo.

What does it cost to build a website?

+

Every project is different. We'll scope your needs during a free consultation and provide a transparent proposal — no hidden fees, no surprises.

How long does a typical project take?

+

Most website builds take 8-16 weeks depending on complexity. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.
