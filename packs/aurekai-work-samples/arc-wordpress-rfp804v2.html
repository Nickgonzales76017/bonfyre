Administrative and Support and Waste Management and Remediation Services Information Other Services (except Public Administration) Professional, Scientific, and Technical Services

**Site registration and application approval must be done prior to submitting a proposal.**

The Atlanta Regional Commission (“ARC”) is soliciting proposals from qualified consultants or firms to provide ongoing WordPress development support and related digital platform services. ARC maintains a diverse ecosystem of WordPress websites, digital services, and web-based tools that support regional planning, communications, public engagement, and organizational operations. ARC seeks a technically mature and operationally reliable partner capable of supporting the continued evolution, maintenance, troubleshooting, accessibility remediation, modernization, enhancement, and design of these systems over the next three years. This support will be on an as-needed basis and will run the course of a year with the option of renewing it annually for two additional years. ARC cannot guarantee that there will be support requests in the coming year and reserves the right not to request any support work over the course of this contract.

**All documents must be combined and submitted as one complete proposal.**
