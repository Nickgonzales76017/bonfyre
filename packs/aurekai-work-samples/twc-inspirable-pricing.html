WordPress care plan FAQ

Everything you need to know about our WordPress care plans, hosting, and ongoing support.

Q

What is a WordPress care plan?

A WordPress care plan is a monthly subscription that bundles managed hosting, backups every 5 hours, security monitoring, malware removal, WordPress core and plugin updates, and technical support into one plan. Also called WP care plans or WordPress maintenance plans, they keep your site secure, fast, and up to date without requiring in-house technical staff. Inspirable's care plans start at $49.99 per month.

Q

How much do WordPress care plans cost?

Inspirable offers four WordPress care plan tiers: Starter at $49.99 per month, Essential at $89.99 per month, Professional at $159.99 per month, and Institutional at $349.99 per month. Annual billing saves approximately 15%. Every plan includes managed hosting, encrypted backups every 5 hours, security scanning and malware removal, enterprise firewall and DDoS protection, global CDN, free SSL, uptime monitoring, and USA-based support.

Q

What is the difference between a WordPress care plan and a WordPress maintenance plan?

They are the same thing. WordPress care plans, WP care plans, WordPress maintenance plans, and WordPress support plans are all industry terms for an ongoing monthly service that keeps a WordPress site secure, updated, backed up, and supported. Inspirable uses "care plan" as the primary term.

Q

What's included in every WordPress care plan?

Every Inspirable WordPress care plan includes managed WordPress hosting, encrypted backups every 5 hours with 30-day retention and one-click restore, security scanning and malware removal, enterprise firewall and DDoS protection, WordPress core, plugin and theme updates, uptime monitoring with 5-minute checks, global CDN, free SSL certificate management, and USA-based support. Essential and Professional tiers add weekly updates, performance reports, database optimization, developer hours, and priority support.

Q

Who are WordPress care plans for?

WordPress care plans are ideal for any organization that needs reliable, professional maintenance without in-house technical staff — from growing small and mid-size businesses (e-commerce, professional services, restaurants, real estate, local retail) to government agencies, credit unions, tribal nations, nonprofits, and other regulated industries. If your site needs to be fast, secure, and supported every day, a care plan fits.

Q

What makes Inspirable different from other WordPress care plan providers?

Unlike most providers, you can actually call us — real USA-based phone support, plus 24/7 monitoring and emergency response. Every plan includes managed hosting, security scanning and malware removal, enterprise firewall and DDoS protection, and backups every 5 hours. We manage over 900 sites across 14 years — from growing businesses to credit unions, tribal nations, and government agencies — so you get enterprise-grade security and support at transparent monthly pricing. Professional plans add staging, speed optimization, and SEO monitoring. And unlike maintenance-only care plan providers, Inspirable is a full design and development agency — the same team can redesign your site or build new features, not just keep it patched.

Q

Do you offer phone support?

Yes — every Inspirable WordPress care plan includes USA-based phone support, on every tier including Starter. Most WordPress care plan providers are ticket- or email-only; with Inspirable you can pick up the phone and reach a real person based in the United States. Plans also include 24/7 monitoring and US-based emergency support for urgent issues.

Q

Are content edits included in WordPress care plans?

Yes. Essential ($89.99/mo), Professional ($159.99/mo), and Institutional ($349.99/mo) plans include unlimited small content edits — text updates, image swaps, link changes, and layout tweaks — handled by our USA-based team. Larger projects such as new pages, custom features, or redesigns are scoped and quoted separately, and development work is covered by your monthly development time (1 hour on Essential, 2 hours on Professional), with additional hours available as needed.

Q

Do WordPress care plans include hosting?

Yes. Every Inspirable WordPress care plan includes fully managed WordPress hosting by default. You do not need a separate hosting account. Our hosting includes encrypted backups every 5 hours with one-click restore, global CDN, free SSL certificates, uptime monitoring with 5-minute checks, enterprise firewall protection, and DDoS mitigation.

Q

Can I get a care plan without hosting, or use my existing host?

Yes. While our plans bundle managed hosting by default, we understand every site is different. If you already have hosting you want to keep, or have other unique requirements, we'll tailor a care plan to fit — covering updates, security, backups, monitoring, and support on your existing infrastructure. Contact us to discuss a custom arrangement.

Q

Can I upgrade my WordPress care plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade from Starter to Essential or Professional at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the remainder of your billing cycle. Contact our team to discuss which tier best fits your needs.

Q

Do you offer staging environments?

Yes. Professional care plan subscribers get a staging environment where we test WordPress core, plugin, and theme updates before applying them to your live site. This prevents update conflicts and downtime. Staging is also available for testing design changes and new functionality before going live.
