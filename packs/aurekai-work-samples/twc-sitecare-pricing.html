Frequently Asked Questions

What if I sign up and I’m not happy with the service?

We want you to be 100% confident in your decision. All of our plans come with a no-risk, 30-day money-back guarantee. If you’re not completely satisfied within the first 30 days, we will issue a full refund.

Am I working with real people or just an automated service?

You are working with a dedicated team of real WordPress professionals. While we use best-in-class tools for monitoring and backups, our support, recommendations, and cleanup services are all handled by our expert team, available to you via phone and email.

What happens if my site gets hacked or goes down?

eace of mind is our specialty. “Malware Cleanup” and “24/7 Security Monitoring” are included in all our plans. If your site is compromised, our team will get to work immediately to clean it and restore it, minimizing downtime and protecting your reputation.

Will you only fix things, or will you help me improve my site?

We aim to be your partner, not just your repairperson. Our Gold and Diamond plans include “Proactive Recommendations” and a “Monthly Site Review.” This means we actively look for opportunities to improve your site’s speed, security, user flow, and overall performance and share those insights with you.

How flexible is billing? Am I locked into a long-term contract?

You have complete flexibility. You can choose to pay month-to-month and cancel at any time. We also offer an annual plan for those who prefer to pay once a year, which includes a discount (two months free) as a thank-you.

What’s the process after I sign up?

Once you choose a plan, we’ll securely collect your website credentials (typically by having you add us as a collaborator) and begin our onboarding process. This includes integrating your site into our 24/7 Security Monitoring, Performance Monitoring, and Daily Backup systems. We’ll have your site protected right away. For Gold & Diamond clients, we quickly move on to optimization and site improvement work. If you transfer to our CoreSpeed hosting on a Gold or Diamond plan, we’ll have your site passing Core Web Vitals and free of all search console critical errors within 14 days.
