0004 Introduction, Purpose and Intent 
0005 The purpose of this invitation to bid is to establish a five (5) year service price agreement for the purchase, customization, production, packaging, and delivery of promotional and outreach items on an as-needed basis for various Dallas County departments.
0006 The awarded Contractor shall provide all labor, supervision, materials, equipment, artwork preparation, proofing, setup, production, packaging, shipping, delivery, and related services necessary to furnish promotional items in accordance with the requirements of this solicitation.
0007 Dallas County anticipates purchasing various promotional and educational items throughout the contract term. Quantities, artwork, specifications, and delivery locations will vary by order and will be identified at the time of purchase.
0008 Specification
0009 Dallas County departments utilize promotional items for public outreach, education, recruitment, employee engagement, health awareness campaigns, special events, and community programs.
